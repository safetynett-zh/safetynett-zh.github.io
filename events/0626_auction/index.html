Renew Roots Art Auction

Renew Roots Art Auction

The event is in support of Renew Roots, a women-led school in Gaza providing free, trauma-informed education. After the school building was destroyed in an IOF strike, classes have continued in temporary tents. Proceeds from the event will directly support the rebuilding of a safe and stable learning space for marginalized and displaced children.

PROGRAM Fri 05.06 19h Vernissage with early access art sale and set by @dj_madonja

Sat 06.06 17h Opening 18h Talk with school founders 18h30 Auction with live music by @anouar_kaddour_cherif 20h30 Afterparty with DJ @mae.be.mad
